Where every naira goes

A business funds a campaign; Ralia turns that budget into real people posting to real audiences — and pays only for the views that actually land. Here's the whole model on one page, in ₦30,000.

1 — Budget becomes reach

Views have a fixed price. Divide the budget by it, and you know exactly how many promoters you hire and how many views you've bought.

2 — You pay for views delivered, not promised

A promoter earns the share they actually deliver. Money left on the table isn't pocketed — it's recycled into more promoters until the target is met.
